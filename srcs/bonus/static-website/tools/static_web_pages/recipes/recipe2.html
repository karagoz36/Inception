Classic Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Butter for cooking

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add milk and egg, and whisk until smooth.
  3. Heat butter in a pan and pour batter to form pancakes.
  4. Cook until golden on both sides and serve with syrup.
