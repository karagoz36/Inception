Fresh Garden Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 lettuce, shredded
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Combine all vegetables in a large bowl.
  2. Drizzle olive oil and vinegar on top.
  3. Season with salt and pepper, and toss well.
  4. Serve fresh as a side dish or appetizer.
